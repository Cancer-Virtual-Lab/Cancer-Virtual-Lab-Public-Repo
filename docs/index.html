Cancer Virtual Lab

A semantic platform that bridges oncological clinical data and research workflows through HL7 FHIR and ontology-driven knowledge graphs.

The Cancer Virtual Lab (CVL) standardises the real-world oncological records of IRCCS Istituto Romagnolo per lo Studio dei Tumori (IRST) "Dino Amadori" as HL7 FHIR resources, lifts them into an RDF knowledge graph governed by a domain ontology, and makes them available for cohort selection, analytics and natural-language querying. It is a collaboration between the University of Bologna, IRST, Aivolution Srl and IFAB.
