CVL-O, the Cancer Virtual Lab Ontology

An OWL 2 ontology for the oncological care pathway of patients from real-world clinical data: diagnosis and TNM staging, therapies and drug administrations, adverse events, biomarkers, genomic variants, laboratory tests, body measurements, symptom assessments, treatment response and clinical services. Aligned with HL7 FHIR, it reuses SNOMED CT, LOINC, NCIt, ICD-10 and ATC.

Namespace http://irst.emr.it/cvl/ Prefix cvl: License CC BY 4.0
